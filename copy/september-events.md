## Notes before you post

**Image prompts avoid naming real artists, brands or films.** Higgsfield will refuse or
produce a bad likeness if you ask for "OneRepublic" or "Devil Wears Prada." The prompts
describe the scene instead, which is also what your existing PACE cards do — photo
background, text overlay carries the name.

**Prices move.** Rilo Kiley has dropped since the screenshot, KETTAMA's Sep 30 sold out
entirely, and Planet X resellers are listing well above face. The screenshot prices are
from Sep 2; verify at the link before you publish a number.

**This is a different mix than your existing PACE lineup.** Your app's events are Venice
and Santa Monica workouts and socials — free or cheap, recurring, small. These are
citywide ticketed concerts and festivals, $20–$229, mostly one-off. Fine as an
"explore" or "what's on in LA" content series; it's a different product than "RSVP on
PACE" if you're not the one holding the guest list.
