your city is already moving. you're just not in the room.

PACE is the app that gets you off the apps. open it, see what's happening near you
this week, tap once, show up. no group chat archaeology. no "we should hang soon."
no scrolling three platforms to find out a run left ten minutes ago.

here's what that looks like right now in venice, santa monica and marina del rey.

mornings start early. LA walk / run club meets at backyard bowls at 8am on saturday —
walk it or run it, nobody checks your pace. long run saturday goes at 8:30 from dell ave.
daybreaker LA turns cinevita into a dance floor at 10am, sober, before most people
are awake. barry's bootcamp venice at 11 if you want someone yelling at you.

middays belong to the beach. beach yoga and beach workout at 2410 ocean front walk.
beach breathwork on venice beach at 11 — bring a mat, they run out. beach tennis
at 3pm friday. beach volleyball saturday at 2:30 with tractfit, teams fill fast.
stretch class at dom dancing space if your hips are done negotiating.

evenings are where you actually meet people. run club by hoka on randolph ave,
6:30pm. tea night at the clubhouse. penmar friday at penmar golf course as the sun
drops. sunset sounds at hume. amplified yoga + DJ at primal moves — yes, a DJ, yes,
during yoga. primal moves x ecstatic on friday at 6:15, which sells out every time.
dance night on electric avenue, no experience needed. breathwork + sound in a small
room in venice. teddy's hot house on sunday at 7 if your idea of a social is a sauna.

sundays wind down. coffee & chill on westminster ave at 10am. superbloom collective
at hotchkiss park. mobbin wednesday every week at 6:30. vuori's private shop on
abbot kinney, invite list only.

that's one week. not a curated highlight reel of a city you don't live in — the actual
calendar, near you, with spots that run out.

three things PACE does that a group chat doesn't:

find it. every run, class, dance, sauna, picnic and rooftop near you on one map.
filter by what you're in the mood for. RSVP in one tap.

see who's going. faces before you walk in, so you're never the person standing by
the door pretending to check your phone. message them first if you want. and
sometimes you meet someone who matches your pace.

host your own. a saturday run, a sunday picnic, a class you've been meaning to teach.
create it in seconds, we help you fill the room.

the fastest way to like where you live is to keep showing up to it. same beach,
same 8am, same faces — that's how strangers turn into your people. PACE just makes
the showing-up part frictionless.

spots are capped on most of these. the good ones close early.
